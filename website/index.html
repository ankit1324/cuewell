Features

Built for the whole loop, from the first call to the final round.

01 / Every round

Every round, one overlay

Auto mode detects the question type and answers in the right shape. Or pin a mode: Coding, Technical, System design, Behavioral or HR.

  • Coding and DSA
  • Technical theory
  • System design
  • Behavioral (STAR)
  • HR
  • Puzzles and estimation

02 / Answers as they stream

Read while it writes

Every answer opens with a one-line reply you can say first, then streams the rest live. Code arrives syntax highlighted, with a copy button.

Say this firstI'll solve it in one pass with a hash map.

03 / Your context

Answers that fit the job

Add the role, company, job description, your resume and notes. They're stored on your Mac and sent to your AI provider with each question, so answers match the job and your experience.

  • Role
  • Company
  • Job description
  • Resume
  • Notes

04 / Voice, locally transcribed

Ask out loud

Press ⌥R and speak. Whisper transcribes on your Mac, and only the text goes to your AI provider. Start and stop by hand, or let pause detection end the question.

05 / Screenshot to answer

Point at the problem

Press ⌘⇧S on a coding prompt or diagram. The image goes straight to the model for visual reasoning, with no OCR step in between.

06 / Privacy mode

Stays off your shared screen

On macOS and Windows, the windows are hidden from most screen capture and screen sharing. Some older browser-based sharing can still see them, and Linux has no protection.

How it works

Three steps. Your hands stay on the keyboard.

  1. 01

    Set it up once

    Pick an AI provider and paste its key, or point it at Ollama. Add your role, company, job description and resume.

    ⌘,Settings
  2. 02

    Ask your way

    Speak the question, capture the screen, or type it into chat.

    ⌥RVoice ⌘⇧SScreenshot ⌘⇧CChat
  3. 03

    Read, then say it

    Start with the one-line reply while the details stream in. Hide everything or let clicks pass through in one keystroke.

    ⌘⇧VShow or hide all ⌥AClick-through

Shortcuts shown for macOS. On Windows, use Ctrl for ⌘ and Alt for ⌥.

Bring your own AI

Your provider, your key, your bill.

Choose a provider in Settings. Keys stay on your Mac in an owner-only file and go only to the provider they belong to.

  • GeminiDefault · text and screenshots
  • ClaudeText and screenshots
  • OpenAIText and screenshots
  • OpenRouterText and screenshots
  • GroqText only
  • DeepSeekText only
  • OllamaRuns locally · no key
  • Keys live in an owner-only file on your Mac. There is no Super Interview server.

Where your data goes

One destination: the AI provider you picked.

No accounts, no telemetry, no Super Interview servers. Here is exactly what leaves your Mac.

What Super Interview stores and what it sends to your AI provider
WhatWhere it livesSent to your AI provider
Typed questions and voice transcripts In memory for the session Sent with the last 15 events of conversation history
Screenshots you capture In memory while the request runs Sent the image itself, without chat history
Interview context: role, company, job description, resume, notes On your Mac Sent with each question, to tailor the answer
Microphone audio Transcribed on your Mac by local Whisper Stays only the text transcript is sent. If you choose Azure Speech instead, audio goes to Azure.
API keys Owner-only file on your Mac Stays used only to sign requests to its own provider
Conversation history In memory. Cleared on restart or with ⌘⇧\ Sent the recent part, with typed and voice questions
Usage analytics None collected Never no telemetry of any kind

With Ollama, requests go to a model on your own machine, so nothing leaves it.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is it really free?

Yes. Super Interview is free and open source under the Apache-2.0 license. You pay your AI provider for what you use, if anything: Gemini offers a free key through Google AI Studio, and Ollama runs models on your own machine.

Which platforms does it support?

macOS 13 (Ventura) or later and 64-bit Windows 10 or later. It's built on Electron. It also runs on Linux from source, but privacy mode does not work there.

Can people on a screen share see the overlay?

On macOS and Windows, the windows are excluded from most screen capture and screen sharing, which use the operating system's capture APIs. Some older browser-based sharing can still capture them, and on Linux they are always visible. Treat privacy mode as a convenience, not a guarantee.

Do I need an API key?

For Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq and DeepSeek, yes: paste it in Settings. Ollama runs locally and needs no key. Groq and DeepSeek accept text only, so screenshots need one of the others.

What does it send, and to whom?

Only the AI provider you choose receives your question or transcript, screenshots you capture, recent history and your interview context. Audio is transcribed on your Mac. See Where your data goes for the full list.

Is it allowed in interviews?

It depends on the interview. Many employers do not allow AI help in live interviews. It's built for practice, mock interviews and settings where AI assistance is permitted. Check the rules first.

How do I install it?

A packaged download is coming soon. Today you can build it from source: clone the repository and run ./setup.sh, which installs dependencies, sets up local Whisper and launches the app. You need Node.js 22.12 or newer.

How is it related to OpenCluely?

Super Interview is a modified version of OpenCluely by TechyCSR, also licensed under Apache-2.0.
