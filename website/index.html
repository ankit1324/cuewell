Everything in Cuewell

The whole feature list, on one page.

What the app does today, grouped so you can scan it. No paid tier and nothing locked.

Answers

  • Auto mode works out the question type: coding, technical theory, system design, behavioral, HR, managerial, puzzles and cases.
  • Fixed modes when you want one shape: Coding, Technical, System design, Behavioral or HR.
  • One line to say first, then the details.
  • Answer length: Auto, Short, Standard or Detailed. Auto is short for voice, standard otherwise.
  • Live streaming into the answer panel, the chat or both.
  • Highlighted code with a copy button on every block.
  • Coding language picker on the toolbar: C++, C, Python, Java or JavaScript.
  • Text and code side by side in the answer panel when an answer has code.

Ways to ask

  • Screenshot of the display under your pointer, on any monitor. The image goes to the model directly, with no OCR step.
  • Voice transcribed on your computer by the built-in whisper.cpp engine, or by Azure Speech. Pause detection or manual start and stop.
  • English, Hindi and Hinglish voice. Hinglish transcripts are written in Roman script.
  • No Python needed for local voice: download a speech model once, Small (190 MB, best for Hindi and Hinglish) or Base (60 MB, faster).
  • Typed chat in its own window.
  • Follow-ups keep context: typed and voice questions include recent history from the session.

Your context

  • Role, company, job description, resume and notes, added in Settings.
  • Stored on your computer in the app data folder, readable only by you on macOS and Linux.
  • Sent with each question to your AI provider, so answers fit the job and your experience.

Control and comfort

  • Settings window with search. Changes save as you make them.
  • Menu bar icon on macOS, tray icon on Windows and Linux: show or hide, screenshot, chat, Settings and Quit.
  • Toolbar menu (the ⋯ button) with About, Settings, updates, logs and Quit.
  • Click-through lets clicks pass to the app underneath. ⌥AOption A
  • Show or hide everything in one keystroke. ⌘⇧VCommand Shift V
  • Global shortcuts for screenshot, voice and chat. ⌘⇧SCommand Shift S, ⌥ROption R, ⌘⇧CCommand Shift C Settings shows any shortcut another app has taken.
  • Stays on top, including over full-screen apps on macOS.
  • Steady windows: they fade in and keep one window level, so they don't blink.
  • Reduced motion is respected when your system asks for it.
  • Window gap between the toolbar and the answer panel, in pixels.

Privacy and security

  • Keys stay on your computer, in a file only you can read on macOS and Linux. Settings shows at most the last 4 characters.
  • No Cuewell servers, no account, no telemetry.
  • History lasts one session. It is kept in memory and cleared when you quit.
  • Locked-down windows: sandboxed pages, per-window permissions and a strict content security policy.
  • Screen-capture protection on macOS and Windows hides the windows from most capture and sharing. Some sharing can still see them, and Linux has none.

Platforms and updates

  • macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.
  • Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit: installer or portable app.
  • Linux, 64-bit: AppImage or .deb.
  • Update notices when a new version is out. Cuewell never installs anything by itself.
  • Crash recovery: a window that stops is reloaded, and errors say what went wrong, such as a bad key, a rate limit or no connection.
  • Free and open source under Apache-2.0, with 239 automated tests.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is it really free?

Yes. Cuewell is free and open source under the Apache-2.0 license. You pay your AI provider for what you use, if anything: Gemini offers a free key through Google AI Studio, and Ollama runs models on your own machine.

Which platforms does it support?

macOS 13 (Ventura) or later on Apple silicon or Intel, 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, and 64-bit Linux (AppImage or .deb). On Linux, privacy mode does not work, so the overlay shows in screen shares. See Download for each file.

How do Windows and Linux differ from macOS?

Shortcuts use Ctrl for ⌘ and Alt for ⌥. There is no menu bar for the overlay, so the app commands (About, updates, Settings, logs, Quit) are in the toolbar's ⋯ menu and the tray icon. Screen-capture protection needs Windows 10 version 2004 or later, and Linux has none. On Linux with Wayland, global shortcuts go through the desktop's shortcuts portal, which may ask you once to allow them, and voice input needs alsa-utils or sox.

What can I change in Settings?

Settings is its own window with sections for General (interview mode, coding language, answer length), AI provider, Interview context, Voice, Shortcuts, Privacy and appearance, and About. A search box finds any setting, and each change saves as you make it. Open it with ⌘,Command Comma, the gear on the toolbar, or the menu bar or tray icon.

Where is the menu bar or tray icon?

On macOS it sits in the menu bar. On Windows and Linux it is in the system tray. Its menu shows or hides Cuewell, takes a screenshot, opens the chat, Settings or Check for Updates…, and quits. On GNOME, tray icons need an AppIndicator extension; Ubuntu includes one.

Does Cuewell update itself?

No. The builds are not code-signed, so Cuewell only tells you when a new release is out. About 15 seconds after launch, at most once a day, it asks GitHub for the latest release and shows a notification if it is newer; click it to open the release page. Check for Updates… checks right away. The request names only the app version. To turn off the automatic check, add UPDATE_CHECKS=off to .env.

Can people on a screen share see the overlay?

On macOS and Windows, the windows are excluded from most screen capture and screen sharing, which use the operating system's capture APIs. Some older browser-based sharing can still capture them, and on Linux they are always visible. Treat privacy mode as a convenience, not a guarantee.

Do I need an API key?

For Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq and DeepSeek, yes: paste it in Settings. Ollama runs locally and needs no key. Groq and DeepSeek accept text only, so screenshots need one of the others.

What does it send, and to whom?

Only the AI provider you choose receives your question or transcript, screenshots you capture, recent history and your interview context. With local voice, audio is transcribed on your computer and never leaves it. See Where your data goes for the full list.

Does voice need Python or an internet connection?

No Python. Cuewell includes the whisper.cpp speech engine. The first time, you download one speech model from the setup wizard or Settings: Small (190 MB, recommended, best for Hindi and Hinglish) or Base (60 MB, faster). After that, voice is transcribed on your computer, even offline. Your AI provider still needs a connection, unless you use Ollama.

Which languages does voice understand?

English, Hindi and Hinglish, or Auto-detect. Pick Hinglish when you mix Hindi and English in one sentence: the transcript comes out in Roman script, such as “Haan, main is approach ko explain karta hoon”, not Devanagari.

Is it allowed in interviews?

It depends on the interview. Many employers do not allow AI help in live interviews. It's built for practice, mock interviews and settings where AI assistance is permitted. Check the rules first.

How do I install it?

Pick the file for your system under Download and open it. The builds are not code-signed yet, so the first launch shows a security warning; each download card says how to get past it. You can also build from source with ./setup.sh (macOS, Linux) or setup.ps1 (Windows).

Packaged downloads for macOS, Windows and Linux are coming soon. Today you can build it from source: clone the repository and run ./setup.sh on macOS or Linux, or setup.ps1 in PowerShell on Windows. The script installs dependencies, fetches the local voice engine and launches the app. You need Node.js 22.12 or newer; on macOS, building the voice engine also needs cmake and the Xcode Command Line Tools.

How is it related to OpenCluely?

Cuewell is a modified version of OpenCluely by TechyCSR, also licensed under Apache-2.0.
