Where your data goes

                        

                        
    
                            
  • No Cuewell server. No account, telemetry or analytics.
    • 
                            
  • Your AI provider gets your questions, screenshots and interview context. With Ollama on this computer, nothing leaves it.
    • 
                            
  • Audio stays on this computer with local Whisper. With Azure Speech it goes to Microsoft.
    • 
                            
  • API keys are stored in .env, readable only by you on macOS and Linux, and go only to the provider they belong to.
    • 
                            
  • Conversation history is kept in memory only and is cleared when Cuewell quits.
    •
